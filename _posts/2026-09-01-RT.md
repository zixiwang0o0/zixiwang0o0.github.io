Today I just dropped by Heibei and visited a friend. To be honest, I don't like his personality. But also, this kind of dislike is not what I have expected. Never imagine a person, go to stay with the one, and your feeling will tell you the truth.

Once again, I realized how smart Aaron is. The **life philosophy** he has taught me, no matter by words or action, is gradually be proved how wise they are. 

## the way we love

Today I suddenly think of something: since Aaron has saved my life and also I treat him as my big brother, so I want to express my love to him. Then I immitated how my parents have done for me. But all these action made Aaron sounded being monitored and felt uncomfortable. Now I gradually understand, maybe my parents didn't use the right way to express their love. We should treat kids like independent human, instead of little child.

## the people we meet

We always interact with the whole world, and a significant process is making new friends. After meeting more and more people, I gradually understand that the relationship between human is somehow a kind of feeling. Just trust your feeling. But there are still some tips: respect others. Trust your intuition.

## How a relationship is built?

Before I thought the answer is to have a deeptalk. Now I take it back. A deeptalk happened may because of impulsy, but the comfort when you stay with someone and also the trust, are the key points.

Also, I may change my idea in the future, but these thoughts stand for me, at this moment.
